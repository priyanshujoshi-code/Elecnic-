Integrated MEP Services

Building Systems, Coordinated for Delivery.

ELECNIC PROJECT PVT. LTD. provides comprehensive Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) contracting solutions for commercial fit-outs, infrastructure and warehouse projects across India. Our expertise covers electrical installations, HVAC and mechanical systems, plumbing and firefighting, and integrated building technologies. We focus on coordinated execution, quality workmanship, reliable installation and efficient building operations.

Electrical Systems

Complete electrical installation and execution including LT panels, distribution boards, power and lighting systems, cable laying, earthing, lightning protection and DG/UPS integration. We deliver reliable electrical infrastructure designed to support safe, efficient and uninterrupted building operations.

HVAC & Mechanical

HVAC and mechanical installation solutions including VRF/VRV systems, DX systems, chillers, AHUs, FCUs and ventilation. Our coordinated execution supports comfortable indoor environments, dependable system performance and efficient building operation.

Plumbing & Firefighting

Comprehensive plumbing and public health engineering solutions including water supply, drainage, piping networks, fire hydrant systems and sprinkler installations. Our execution focuses on dependable building services, coordinated installation and fire safety requirements.

ELV & IBMS

Integrated low-voltage and building automation solutions including fire alarm systems, CCTV surveillance, access control, structured cabling, public address systems and IBMS. Our installations support building security, communication, centralized monitoring and operational efficiency.
